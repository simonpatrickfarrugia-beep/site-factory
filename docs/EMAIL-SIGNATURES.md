Logo mark per brand: `brands/<brand>/public/email/signature-mark.png` (96×96, shown at 40×40), served at `https://<domain>/email/signature-mark.png` once deployed.

Used by: Claude's outreach drafts (signature in the body), the Exchange mail-flow rule per brand (stamps it when the From address is that brand and the body doesn't already contain the marker line), and named signatures in Outlook web.
