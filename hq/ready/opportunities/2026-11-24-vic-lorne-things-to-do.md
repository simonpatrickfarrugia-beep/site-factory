## What the page is missing (new `body` paragraphs in `data/vic/content.json` → lorne, shown on /lorne)

**Para 1 (direct answer):** Most things to do in Lorne are within 3 km of the main street: the beach and pier, a ring of lookouts
in the hills behind town, and waterfall walks in the Otways. Our list has 20 things to do.

**Para 2 (beach and town):** Lorne Beach runs right along the town, with the Lorne Sea Baths at 81 Mountjoy Parade and Lorne Pier
about 1.3 km from the centre. Qdos gallery is about 900 m out, and Lorne Golf Course about 1.6 km.

**Para 3 (lookouts and waterfalls):** Teddy's Lookout, about 1.5 km from the centre, is the famous view of the Great Ocean Road
curving along the coast. For a walk, the Canyon and Phantom Falls Hiking Trail takes in The Canyon and Phantom Falls, about 2.5–3 km
from town; Sheoak Falls Lookout and Swallow Cave Lookout are about 3 km out. Erskine Falls is a drive up into the hills.

**Para 4 (groups and kids):** Live Wire Park (180 Erskine Falls Road, about 1.2 km from the centre, open 9:45am–5pm daily) is the
pick for a group or family activity. There are two playgrounds and Lions Park for a picnic.
