# Niche discovery (weekly)

Updated 2026-10-24. Every Sunday Claude proposes new site ideas and each is screened for **lots of searches with little competition**: related Google searches that contain the topic's own words, with 100+ a month **and** SEO difficulty 25 or less. 12 ideas screened this week for about US$0.24.

- **Easy searches**: how many related searches pass that bar (a site needs many pages, so breadth matters).
- **Opportunity**: easy volume, weighted by money (CPC) and breadth. Use it to rank, not as a forecast.
- **Domains**: names checked for promoted ideas (✅ not registered, ❌ taken). Re-checked before buying; buy only once an idea passes scoring and competition.
- ⭐ = cleared the bar (25+ easy searches and 30,000+/mo in the US or 8,000+/mo in Australia) and went into the full niche scoring (`hq/reports/niches.md`) and the competition check.
